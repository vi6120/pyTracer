## Modified versions and forks

The open-source licenses let you modify and redistribute the code, but you may
**not** call your modified version "pyTracer." Give your fork its own distinct
name. You may factually state that it is "derived from pyTracer" or "a fork of
pyTracer," as long as you do not imply it is the official project.

## Naming integrations and plugins

Name your own project first and describe the relationship. For example, call it
"Acme Dashboard for pyTracer," not "pyTracer Dashboard." Do not make "pyTracer"
the leading or dominant part of your name.

## Logo

Once an official logo is published: do not alter its colors, proportions, or
elements, keep clear space around it, and do not use it as your own icon or in a
way that implies endorsement.

## General guidelines

- Use "pyTracer" as an adjective with a generic noun where practical (for example
  "the pyTracer SDK"), not as a verb or in the plural.
- Keep the capitalization as "pyTracer."
- Where reasonable, include a notice: "pyTracer is a trademark of vi6120."
