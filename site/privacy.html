Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Template notice: this policy is a starting draft, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer and adjusted to your actual data practices and the laws that apply to you (for example GDPR and CCPA) before you rely on it.

This Privacy Policy explains how vi6120 ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the pyTracer website at pytracer.com (the "Site") and the hosted pyTracer service (the "Service"). The open-source pyTracer software that you run yourself is covered by its licenses, not by this policy; when you self-host, you are the controller of the data you process.

Information we handle

The website

The Site is a static site. We do not require an account to browse it and we do not use tracking cookies for advertising. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send (such as your email address and message).

The hosted service

  • Account information you provide to use the Service, such as your name, email address, and organization.
  • Trace data that you send to the Service through the SDK or gateway: the records of what your agents do (LLM calls, tool calls, reasoning steps, metadata such as branch and commit). This data may include content that you or your end users provide.
  • Operational data such as API request metadata and logs needed to run and secure the Service.

Redaction happens before data leaves your systems

The pyTracer SDK applies a redaction ruleset (for secrets and common personal data patterns) to span content at capture time, before anything is transmitted. You control the rules and remain responsible for what you choose to send. We recommend not sending data you do not need to capture.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To store and return your trace data to you and your authorized team.
  • To communicate with you about the Service and respond to your requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

For the hosted Service, we handle your trace data on your behalf as a data processor; you remain the controller of that data.

Storage, retention, and security

Trace and mock data are stored in our databases. Data in transit is protected with TLS, and we apply access controls and the hardening measures described in our security documentation. You can configure retention to age out old traces; when you delete data or close your account, we delete or de-identify the associated data within a reasonable period, subject to legal retention needs.

Sharing and subprocessors

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Service (for example cloud hosting, content delivery, and DNS providers), under agreements that limit their use of it, and where required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise them, contact us at the address below. If you are an end user whose data was sent to us by a pyTracer customer, please contact that customer, who controls the data.

International transfers

We may process and store data in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Children

The Service is not directed to children and is not intended for use by them.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the updated version here and revise the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@pytracer.com.
